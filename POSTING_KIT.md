## Full description (README / portfolio / LinkedIn post)
Autocorrect Tool v20 is Project #03 of my 100+ AI Projects series — built advanced from the start, never a basic v1.

It corrects a single word with live Top-5 suggestions, or a whole sentence at once, using Levenshtein distance plus 80+ common misspellings. v20 also fixes split words (alot → a lot), double words (the the → the), lone i → I, sentence capitals and spacing, and shows every change it made. You can add your own words to a personal dictionary, copy, share or export the corrected text, and switch light/dark theme. It works fully offline on your phone and installs as an app, and the Python version has the exact same v20 logic.
